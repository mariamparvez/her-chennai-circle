This Privacy Policy explains how Her Chennai Circle collects and uses information when you enquire about or attend one of our events.

What we collect

When you use our registration form, we may collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, and event-related preferences. We may also receive information you share when you contact us on WhatsApp.

How we use it

We use this information to process your event interest or registration, communicate event details, answer your questions, and improve future Her Chennai Circle gatherings. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing information

We share information only with trusted services needed to operate our registration and communications, or where required by law. Our registration form is hosted by Google Forms, which has its own privacy practices.

Advertising

We may use information from website interactions to measure and improve our advertising, including through Meta advertising tools where enabled. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage advertising preferences through your browser and platform settings.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the information we hold about you, or opt out of non-essential event communications, by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 73584 00469.

Contact
